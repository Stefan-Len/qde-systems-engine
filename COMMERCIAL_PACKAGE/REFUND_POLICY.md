# Refund Policy

## 1. Digital Source-Code Product

QDE ES Deterministic Execution Infrastructure Package is a digital source-code
package.

The Delivered Package may include source code, documentation, tests, scripts,
configuration examples, prompts, package manifest information, and related
digital files.

Because source code can be copied, inspected, modified, stored, and reused after
delivery, it cannot realistically be returned in the same way as a physical
product.

## 2. Final Sale After Digital Delivery

Purchases are generally final once access to the Delivered Package has been
provided, the package has been made available for download, or the buyer has
received repository or archive access.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, where the Author requires a
pre-delivery form, checkout acknowledgement, electronic signature flow, or
sales-channel confirmation, delivery should not begin until the buyer has
completed and accepted it. That acceptance should identify the product as digital
content, request immediate digital delivery, and acknowledge that any withdrawal
or cooling-off right may be lost once delivery or access begins.

This policy does not limit or exclude any mandatory rights that cannot be
limited or excluded under applicable law.

## 3. When Refunds May Be Considered

A refund may be considered only in limited cases, such as:

- the buyer was charged but did not receive access to the Delivered Package;
- the delivered archive is corrupt or unreadable and the issue cannot be
  corrected by re-delivery;
- the delivered package materially differs from the delivery scope or package
  manifest;
- the unmodified delivered package does not run in an environment that meets
  the documented minimum requirements, and the issue cannot be resolved by
  re-delivery or reasonable correction;
- there was a duplicate charge or clear payment-processing error;
- the sales channel requires a refund under its own mandatory terms;
- applicable mandatory law requires a refund.

If an issue can be reasonably corrected by re-delivery of the package,
replacement of a corrupted archive, or clarification of access instructions, the
Author may choose that correction before considering a refund.

## 4. What Is Not a Refund Reason

The following are not refund reasons after digital delivery:

- the buyer changed their mind;
- the buyer no longer wants to use the Software;
- the buyer expected custom implementation work;
- the buyer expected production LIVE broker integration to be included;
- the buyer expected broker-backed PAPER setup to be included;
- the buyer does not have a broker account, Databento subscription, API key, or
  required vendor access;
- the buyer's local environment does not meet the documented minimum
  requirements;
- the buyer cannot complete a buyer-side broker adapter or custom market-data
  integration;
- the buyer modified the Software and the modified version does not work as
  expected;
- the Software does not produce profit;
- the buyer experiences trading losses, drawdown, account breach, funded-account
  challenge failure, or live trading issues;
- the buyer disagrees with the architecture, implementation style, strategy
  design, model behavior, or risk policy after receiving the source code;
- the buyer expected financial advice, trading advice, trading signals,
  profitability support, custom implementation help, private coaching, broker
  setup help, response-time commitment, or managed trading service.

## 5. No Return of Source Code

If a refund is approved or required, the buyer must stop using the Delivered
Package and delete local copies where legally and practically required.

However, because copied source code cannot be reliably returned or verified as
deleted, refund eligibility remains limited to the cases described in this
policy, the applicable sales channel terms, and mandatory law.

## 6. Sales Channel Processing

Refunds, if approved or required, are processed through the original sales
channel where technically possible.

For purchases made through Lemon Squeezy or another sales channel, that
platform's payment, tax, chargeback, refund, and dispute-processing terms may
also apply.

The Author may require the buyer's order number, license key, delivery
reference, buyer email, and package version before reviewing any refund request.

Refund review requests must be sent by email to:

`stefanlen@qde-systems.com`

approved.
